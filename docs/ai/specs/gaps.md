everything (D264's "not now" for them is dropped; screentips stay deferred); then every package below. The rules for a package are
the engineering campaign's (`docs/ai/specs/engineering.md` section 2) and its merging (section 3); packages run as agents in their
own worktrees (`.claude/worktrees/<id>`), at most 3 at once on disjoint files. Every package ticks its `docs/ai/PORT-CHECKLIST.md` rows
and its windows in section 13 and, after its regen, recounts the checklist's numbers with
`python3 tools/checklist/refresh-port-checklist.py`; the lead reruns it at each merge train's end (the owner, 2026-11-19).
