# Anomaly Station Decision Log

## Project mission (primary objective, D81)

**Anomaly Station is NOT a remake and NOT an "inspired-by game" of Space Station 13. The goal: a full PORT of SS13 (tgstation codebase, snapshot `1d99bd7f`) to 3D in first person, reproducing the mechanics, systems, and behavior of the original (D81, clarified by D86).**

- The reference-first principle (D86): we write our own mechanics as little as possible and reuse the ready-made tg reference code as much as possible (formulas, numbers, logic, order of events) — this speeds up development and reduces the number of errors.
- Incidental optimization of systems and algorithms without changing the core logic is allowed, as are deviations required by the specifics of the 3D world and first person. A strict "exactly as is, bug for bug" is NOT required.
- Significant deviations from the core logic or mechanics are recorded in a lightweight deviation registry.
- The vertical slice and the alpha are stages on the way to the full port, not ends in themselves; the "parity is not a goal" conclusion from the research (SS14 took the path of reimagining) does NOT apply to this project.

Decisions are made by the owner and recorded here as the port plan is refined.
Research base: `docs/ai/research/`. Final plan: `docs/ai/PLAN.md`.

| D23 | Network: chunks and visibility | We introduce a chunk system for REPLICATION (interest management, similar in idea to Minecraft): the client is sent only the dynamic chunk data within the player's area of interest (objects, mobs, visual atmosphere state), smoothly and unnoticeably (with a radius margin and hysteresis).
