# 03. Other ports, runtimes and SS13 successors

Research date: 2026-10-05. Scope: all attempts other than the BYOND version itself: OpenDream and other DM runtimes, remakes on other engines (Unity, Unreal, Godot, Source, Bevy, VR, etc.), and 3D games inspired by SS13.

## Summary

- Over 15 years, more than 30 attempts and successors are known: remakes, runtimes, inspired-by games. Only Space Station 14 (SS14) achieved mass success: 2D, its own C# engine, hundreds of contributors. Unitystation (Unity, 2D) has been alive for 10 years but has still not reached Early Access on Steam.
- We did not find a fully playable 3D port of SS13 ⚠️ (this is a claim of absence: the search was incomplete, see Gaps; a playable 3D "inspired-by" successor does exist: SpessVR on itch). SS3D (Unity) has remained pre-alpha for 7 years. SSNT (Rust/Bevy), SpessVR (Unity, VR) and several Godot projects are either tiny or have no release date.
- Commercial 3D attempts ended for different reasons: Primulus was abandoned after a failed Kickstarter; Centration was abandoned and removed from Steam in 2015 (the Kickstarter in the sources does not refer to it, see Verification); Dean Hall's Ion was cancelled in 2017 (Hall moved, RocketWerkz switched to other projects, the collaboration with Improbable ended). Commercial successes (Stationeers, Barotrauma) did not port SS13 but took its ideas and narrowed the scope.
- OpenDream (a DM runtime in C#) already runs tgstation, but with defects, and its client is two-dimensional. It is the only real way to preserve 1.2 million lines of DM logic, but for 3D we would have to write the client ourselves.
- In 2017-2019 SS14 tried Godot 3 specifically for 2D+3D and moved to its own renderer. The reasons (Mono crashes, no batching, an inflexible renderer) apply to Godot 3.x; for Godot 4.7 they need to be rechecked with a prototype rather than taken on faith.

## Findings

### 1. Early remakes 2011-2016: the graveyard ("The Curse")
The BeeStation wiki lists: Standalone SS13 (C#/XNA, 2011-2015, developed by Goonstation, closed in January 2015 and released as open source), a C++/OpenGL port of Baystation12 (2011), Java/LWJGL (2011, "technical reasons"), "SS13 in 3D" on GLUT/C (2011), Space Station: Source (discussion only), UnitySS (a very brief attempt), Minecraft SS13 (got as far as graphics and assets). All abandoned. In the community, "SS13 remakes" became an inside joke.
Source: https://wiki.beestation13.com/view/List_of_remakes (mirror https://nsv.beestation13.com/wiki/List_of_remakes). Confidence: medium (community wiki, secondary source; primary links unavailable).

### 2. Space Station 14: the only mass success, and its path through Godot
- The Standalone SS13 code was released in 2015 (RobustToolbox was created on GitHub 2015-01-17), by 2016 the project had almost died of burnout, and in 2017 it was revived by PJB3005 and friends because it had advanced further than their own probes in Unity, Unreal, C++ and Rust. Source: https://spacestation14.com/post/25-01-19-10-years-of-space-station-14/ (high).
- The goal of 2017-2018 was 2D and 3D at once. Writing their own 3D renderer on OpenGL proved beyond their means, so in December 2017 they opened a Godot 3 branch (C# via Mono) and merged it into the main one in April 2018. Source: https://spacestation14.com/post/18-02-26-progress-report-5/ and https://spacestation14.com/post/18-04-13-progress-report-6/ (high).
- As early as February 2019 the developers announced their own renderer, Clyde. Reasons: frequent crashes related to Mono; architectural conflicts solved by "hacks" that reached the point of having to distribute their own Godot binaries; no sprite batching (about 30 FPS on integrated graphics with lighting; Clyde gets roughly 2.5 times higher FPS, but compared with Godot WITHOUT lighting, so the comparison is not "like for like"). Source: https://spacestation14.com/post/19-02-20-progress-report-10/ (high).
- In May 2019 Godot support was removed entirely: an inflexible renderer, weak High-DPI support in the UI, and support that "hurt more and more". Source: https://spacestation14.com/post/19-05-12-progress-report-15/ (high). These complaints apply to Godot 3.x; their relevance to 4.7 has not been checked.
- Networking: in 2018 SS14 reworked entity netcode because the full serialized state (blobs of about 50 KB) with diffing at 60 Hz ate the CPU; after the fix the client load dropped by half and the server load by about 6 times. Source: https://spacestation14.com/post/18-06-24-progress-report-7/ (high).
- SS14 today: 3.8k stars, about 38k commits (rechecked), 408 contributors per the GitHub API (with a linked account) and a push on 2026-10-05 (⚠️ not rechecked: the repository page does not show these numbers); the RobustToolbox engine (ECS, Lidgren) is separated from content. Licenses: SS14 code is MIT, assets mostly CC BY-SA 3.0, some CC BY-NC-SA 3.0; RobustToolbox code is MIT only starting from 2019-03-13, earlier code is under GPLv3 (the engine's legal.md) ⚠️ "just MIT" is inaccurate. On Steam it is still "Playtest / Coming soon to Early Access". Sources: https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14, https://github.com/space-wizards/RobustToolbox, https://store.steampowered.com/app/1255460/Space_Station_14/ (high). Peak online figures are contradictory (4 701 vs 21 444 on different trackers), do not use (low).

### 3. Why SS14 survived: factors according to the developers themselves
- Reliance on the whole SS13 community (coders, admins, hosts), not on a small core; acceptance of incomplete parity with SS13. Source: 10 Years of SS14 (above), high.
- In 2021, four years in, SS14 had about 15% of SS13's content (PJB3005's estimate); in 2022 it overtook tgstation by number of merges over 30 days (520 vs 326 PRs). Sources: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=29108648 and https://spacestation14.com/post/22-07-27-progress-report-36/ (medium: self-assessment).
- SS14 is deliberately more complex than a simple port: pixel (continuous) movement, rotating grids (stations/shuttles), its own engine (comment from an HN user, not necessarily a contributor; medium).
- Scale problems remain: thousands of players, hundreds of contributors, organizational load (high).
- Side effect: forks and spin-offs are built on RobustToolbox, for example RMC-14 (an inspired-by game based on Colonial Marines on the SS14 engine; the 42k commits in the repository include inherited SS14/RobustToolbox history ⚠️ not suitable as a measure of its own activity). Source: https://github.com/RMC-14/RMC-14 (high).

### 4. Unitystation: Unity 2D, 10 years without a Steam release
Started in November 2016 by cloning the tgstation codebase as of ~2017 into Unity, first public alpha in February 2020. Now: 734 stars, about 20k commits, 218 contributors, Unity 6000.2, AGPL, push 2026-10-03, version 0.5, "Coming soon" on Steam. The last blog post came out more than a year ago, and the authors mentioned burnout. On Patreon at the time of the snapshot there were 14 paying members out of 111 participants. Sources: https://github.com/unitystation/unitystation, https://unitystation.org, https://store.steampowered.com/app/801140/Unitystation/, https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2020/02/unitystation-a-cross-platform-remake-of-space-station-13-has-a-first-public-build-up/page=1/, https://www.patreon.com/unitystation/about. Confidence: high for metrics, medium for the reasons for the delay.

### 5. SS3D (RE:SS3D): the main 3D attempt, 7 years of pre-alpha
- The original *beep (Unity, early 2018) advanced quickly and vanished without a trace; the community resurrected the project (repository since 2019-08). Sources: https://ss3d.space/about/, https://wiki.beestation13.com/view/List_of_remakes (high).
- State: 292 stars, 55 contributors, release 0.0.7.6 from April 2025, 7 contributors in the 0.0.7.6 release notes (rechecked; "4-7 active" is an estimate), and a joking tagline in the devblog index about a "Discord with a month without news" (⚠️ this is not a complaint but a joke; the text of the posts was not read). Date of 0.0.7.6: the devblog gives 2025-04, the GitHub release page shows "9 Apr" without a year (⚠️ the year is not confirmed by the release). The README still calls the project pre-alpha. Sources: https://github.com/RE-SS3D/SS3D, https://github.com/RE-SS3D/SS3D/releases, https://ss3d.space/devblog/ (high).
- Technical traces of difficulty: the network layer changed from Mirror to FishNet (the FAQ speaks of Mirror, the dev guide of FishNet; https://ss3d.space/faq/, https://ss3d.gitbook.io/dev-guide/); the atmosphere system was lost during a branch transition, its "resurrection" is priced at a $60 bounty per task (issue #1464); the whole asset catalog is loaded into memory at startup (~600 MB, issue #1494); bugs with network errors during construction are open. Sources: https://github.com/RE-SS3D/SS3D/issues/1464, /issues/1494 (medium-high).
- License: code MIT, art CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial), meaning the art cannot be used in a paid game. Camera: angled top-down with rotation; first person and VR are not part of the first release. Source: https://ss3d.space/faq/ (high).
- The henkhooft/SS3D fork on Unity 6 rewrites interactions, inventory, health, atmos and the map editor, because upstream cannot keep up with review; it has 0 stars and can be played only from the editor. Source: https://github.com/henkhooft/SS3D (medium).

### 6. SSNT: leaving Unity for Rust/Bevy
SS-NT/ssnt (created 2021-07, push 2026-07, 192 commits, 18 stars, AGPL) is made by a former SS3D maintainer who left because, according to the README, Unity is closed and commercially controlled, and the engine "will hold back" the project. It uses SS3D models and textures. Networked play only via a local server, "very early pre-alpha". Source: https://github.com/SS-NT/ssnt (high).

### 7. Godot attempts: none with a notable team
A GitHub search found only: Space-Station-Theta (Godot 4, C#, PS1-style 3D, AGPL, repository since December 2025, push 2026-10-03, 2 contributors, 4 stars, "no interesting gameplay", they themselves write that they lack developers), Malbios/SpaceStation13D (solo vertical slice, 3 commits), anomalou/darkstone (2D SS13-like, 43 commits), SS13-IDLE-GODOT and hotline-station (toys). On the Godot forum there is a thread about an SS13-style atmosphere simulation on a 64x64 grid that crashes because data is stored in nodes and string keys; the advice: keep data separate from the scene tree and use queued updates. Sources: https://github.com/search?q=%22space+station+13%22+godot&type=repositories, https://github.com/IlifSine/Space-Station-Theta, https://github.com/Malbios/SpaceStation13D, https://github.com/anomalou/darkstone, https://forum.godotengine.org/t/ss13-based-atmospheric-system-need-help-to-optimize/79139 (high for the facts; the conclusion "no precedent" is an interpretation).

### 8. Commercial 3D remakes of SS13: Centration, Primulus, TechCompliant, Ion
- Centration (Angry Engineers Entertainment, a studio founded by an early SS13 developer; first person): first Unity with multiplayer (that version was shelved over supposed technical limits of the engine), then a remake on Unreal without working multiplayer; on Steam Early Access since April 2014, the GamingOnLinux author noted an almost complete absence of gameplay; officially abandoned in 2015 and unavailable on Steam. ⚠️ The "Kickstarter failed" version from the first edition of the report is not confirmed by sources: the BeeStation wiki mentions a Kickstarter failure only for Primulus and says nothing about Kickstarter for Centration; removed. Sources: https://wiki.beestation13.com/view/List_of_remakes, https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2014/04/centration-fps-space-simulation-released-on-steam-for-linux (high).
- Primulus: two Baystation12 developers, Unity, top-down 3D, a single-player tech demo (3D environment, atmosphere simulation, object interaction); abandoned after a failed Kickstarter. Source: BeeStation (medium, rechecked).
- TechCompliant: 3D on Unreal with DCPU elements, attention from Notch; fate not confirmed (low; only a search snippet about treeform's Medium article, the article itself is unavailable).
- Dean Hall's Ion (an SS13-style MMO on SpatialOS/Improbable): announced in 2015 (⚠️ E3 specifically not rechecked against sources), cancelled on 7 March 2017. Reasons per PCGamesN: Hall moved to New Zealand, RocketWerkz switched to other projects, the collaboration with Improbable ended; Hall said the game is possible only with a company like Improbable. This is a studio project with a partner, not "solo without money". Source: https://www.pcgamesn.com/dayz/dean-hall-mmo-ion-cancelled (high, rechecked).

### 9. Commercial "successors" that took ideas, not a port
- Stationeers (RocketWerkz, Unity, 3D): Early Access since December 2017, still EA, 88% positive out of 5 958 reviews; focused on engineering and atmosphere, no traitor/antagonist mechanics (per a Steam thread, "not yet"; this is not "removed from SS13", they simply were not made); a designer confirms SS13's influence on the "two hands" inventory. Sources: https://store.steampowered.com/app/544550/Stationeers/, https://steamcommunity.com/app/544550/discussions/0/4339860800345738997/ (high).
- Barotrauma (Undertow Games, MonoGame, 2D): in development since 2014, EA in June 2019, release on 13 March 2023, 95% out of 28 836 reviews; the lead developer called SS13 the main source of inspiration and criticized its interface. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barotrauma_(video_game), https://store.steampowered.com/app/602960/Barotrauma/ (high).
- Meeple Station (Vox Games, Unity): "with a hint of SS13", Kickstarter for 40 000 AUD, at the time of the article (2018-09-06) about 2 000 raised (rechecked); ⚠️ "released in EA in February 2019" is not confirmed by the article's source. Source: https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2018/09/meeple-station-the-cooperative-space-station-builder-is-now-on-kickstarter-linux-demo-available/page=1/ (medium).
- Adjacent games with no link to SS13: Lethal Company (Unity, solo developer, ~10 million copies in 3 months, 4 players, proximity voice chat), Void Crew (1-6 players, 88% out of 5 114). Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethal_Company, https://store.steampowered.com/app/1063420/Void_Crew/ (high; no direct link to SS13).
- SpessVR (Unity, VR+desktop, physical bodies, voice): playable for free on itch (devlog "we are on itch" from 2020-09-17, page publication date 2020-11-30 ⚠️ "September 2020" is not exact), still "Coming soon to Early Access" on Steam with no date. Sources: https://store.steampowered.com/app/898200/Spess/, https://badbons.itch.io/spess (high).

### 10. OpenDream: a DM runtime that already runs tgstation
- C# (.NET 10), client and server on RobustToolbox, MIT (per the repository page; the README itself does not name the license), since September 2020, 66 contributors and a push on 2026-09-24 (⚠️ not rechecked), 282 stars. The README shows screenshots of /tg/station, Paradise, Goonstation (that is, "runs" is confirmed only by screenshots, not by our own run), but honestly calls the project early: "expect bugs and missing features". The client and server are incompatible with BYOND. Sources: https://github.com/OpenDreamProject/OpenDream, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenDreamProject/OpenDream/master/README.md (high).
- Differences from BYOND: no hub features, DLLs must be rebuilt for 64 bits, UTF-16 instead of UTF-8, but there is no 4 GB limit, native Linux, the Tracy profiler, a debugger and hot reload. Source: https://github.com/OpenDreamProject/OpenDream/wiki/Differences-Between-OpenDream-and-BYOND (high).
- Check against the local tgstation clone (HEAD of 2026-10-04): CI (`.github/workflows/run_linters.yml`) downloads only the OpenDream compiler (DMCompiler) and compiles `tgstation.dme` with `--suppress-unimplemented` (that is, CI only builds the code and does not run the game, and the flag hides warnings about unimplemented features: a successful build is not parity); the code has `OPENDREAM_REAL`/`OPENDREAM` conditional-compilation branches: dreamluau is disabled (byondapi is not supported), tracy, `count_atoms` and some debug verbs do not work (`count_datums` was not checked separately). Code scale (recounted 2026-10-05): 7 476 .dm files in `code/` (7 494 in the whole tree), 1 226 880 .dm lines in `code/` (about 1.23 million), 1 448 .dmi in `icons/` (another 9 in `tools/`), 632 .dmm maps in `_maps/` (including test ones, shuttles, ruins), ⚠️ corrected: `sound/` has 4 423 files, but only 4 326 of them are audio (3 793 ogg, 531 mid, 1 mp3, 1 mod), the rest is 95 txt and 2 md files with attributions; outside `sound/` there are about 11 more ogg files in `config/reboot_themes/` (high, local check).
- Open tgstation-on-OD problems, judging by the tracker: initialization order (#2713), client lighting rendering and invisible objects (#2509, #2506); closed: clothing flicker, formatting crashes. Source: https://github.com/OpenDreamProject/OpenDream/issues?q=is%3Aissue+tgstation (medium). Wizard's Den runs a VM "OpenDream1" (purpose not disclosed, https://docs.spacestation14.com/en/community/infrastructure-reference/wizards-den-infrastructure.html). There are no mentions of Godot in the tracker (a search for "godot" gave 0 results, medium).

### 11. Other runtimes and ports "through code"

The tracker has an emotional dispute with an SS14 developer about code quality (issue #76), that is, competing projects spent effort on each other.
- Griefly (C++/Qt client, Go server, Python scripts, 2013-2019, 2 375 commits, 126 stars) is frozen: https://github.com/griefly/griefly, https://osgameclones.com/space-station-13 (medium).
- ss13remake (C#, Lidgren): after the code was opened in 2015 the author's original sprites stayed with him and were not handed over, and were replaced with placeholders. https://github.com/ss13remake/ss13remake (high).
- Small stuff: OpenMud, TCL4DM, OSS-13 (C++/Python), Space Schmucks (Rust; the repository returns 404, low).

### 12. Community and reasons for the breakdown: what participants say
- Fragmentation: SS13 is not one game but several competing codebases. Per an HN thread from 2021 (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=29108648; medium, opinions) one commenter considers the "curse" lifted only when established servers spend at least a year on SS14; ⚠️ this is one user's criterion, not an established fact that the Goon/tg servers "will not move".
- Key BYOND semantics, recalled by the tgstation host MrStonedOne: cooperative single-threaded `sleep()`/`spawn()` with stack detachment on "soft locks". Any manual rewrite of DM logic must reproduce this behavior (coroutines/async), otherwise doors, animations and timers will break (medium, opinion; same thread).
- Upstream drift: Unitystation cloned tg as of ~2017, Bluespess is pinned to one commit, whereas tgstation merges 300+ PRs a month (tg: 326 merges/30 days in 2022). A port of a snapshot goes stale immediately (medium, an inference from two sources).

## Implications for the 3D port

1. Scope is the main killer. Nobody has taken a 3D SS13 even to release; SS14 with a shared community got about 15% of the content in 4 years. We need an explicit scope ladder: a vertical slice (1 station, ~6 roles, atmos, electricity, death/healing), then expansion. Parity with tg as a goal should be ruled out.
2. Keep the simulation core separate from Godot (this is already in the plans) and treat Godot as a replaceable client. Engines in these histories changed for everyone: SS14 (SFML, Godot, its own), SSNT (Unity, Bevy), SS3D (Mirror, FishNet), Centration (Unity, Unreal). Do not store data in the scene tree (the lesson of the atmosphere thread on the Godot forum and of SS14 itself).
3. SS14's experience with Godot 3 cannot be carried over directly to 4.7, but it suggests what to check with a prototype first: C# stability, rendering a large number of entities, renderer flexibility, UI at high DPI, replacing Godot's high-level networking or wrapping it thinly.
4. Design networking in from day one: Centration never lived to see multiplayer, SS3D has network bugs during construction, SS14 reworked its netcode (serialization and diffing of the full state at 60 Hz ate the CPU; after the fix the client is half and the server about 6 times lighter; ⚠️ "the main bottleneck" is not claimed by the source). We need an authoritative server, interest management (PVS) and prediction.
5. The choice between "runtime" and "rewrite" must be made deliberately. The OpenDream-server plus own Godot client option preserves DM logic, but DM atoms are two-dimensional (icon/icon_state, the .dmm grid): models, heights and 3D collisions would have to be added as a separate data layer, and the OpenDream client protocol is tied to RobustToolbox. The "C# from scratch" option (Unitystation/SS14) gives a clean 3D model but costs many years and a community. A short spike of both options is needed before choosing.
6. The art pipeline is the bottleneck specifically in 3D. SS3D failed to accumulate content in 7 years; art under CC BY-NC-SA rules out commercial use; for ss13remake the sprites were not handed over at all. Decide the asset license and a modular station kit in advance; low-poly/PS1 stylization (Space-Station-Theta, Lethal Company) lowers the cost.
7. Licenses: tgstation is under AGPL-3.0 (code after 2014-12-31; earlier code GPLv3; TGS DMAPI under MIT) and CC BY-SA 3.0 (assets "unless otherwise stated"); checked locally against README/LICENSE. ⚠️ But "unless otherwise stated" is substantial: in the attribution files of `sound/` there are at least 28 lines with sounds under CC BY-NC 3.0/4.0 ("Attribution NonCommercial") and one track under CC-BY-NC-SA-3.0 (`sound/music/antag/pirate/`), meaning even a direct transfer of tgstation assets is not clean for a commercial release; a line-by-line asset audit is needed. A derivative work probably inherits the AGPL/CC BY-SA obligations. SS14: code MIT, assets mostly CC BY-SA 3.0 with some CC BY-NC-SA 3.0; RobustToolbox MIT only since 2019-03-13. NC assets (as in SS3D) prohibit commercial use, and AGPL requires opening the source; a paid release needs a legal assessment.
8. Organizationally, most failures are a solo developer or a small team without stable funding (*beep, Primulus, ss13remake). ⚠️ Centration (the Angry Engineers studio) and Ion (RocketWerkz + Improbable) do not fit this generalization: the first closed because of technical limits and unfinished multiplayer, the second was cancelled because of the studio's change of priorities and the partner's departure. Success came to those who either relied on a ready-made community (SS14) or had studio funding (Stationeers, Barotrauma), but being a studio did not by itself prevent failures. Bounty systems (SS3D: $60 per task; Unitystation: 14 paying patrons) do not provide money for a stable team.
9. Design decision for 3D: all successful 3D analogs dropped part of SS13. Stationeers has no antagonist mechanics ("not yet" per a Steam thread); Barotrauma shrank the station to a submarine for 16 players and a 2D side view. Social deduction and role-play survived in 2D (SS14). We must decide what in SS13 is the core of the 3D game: systemic simulation, social sabotage, or both.
10. Community infrastructure: SS14 has a launcher, hosting (Wizard's Den), and a split between engine and content for third-party servers; this is a notable part of its success and should be designed in (content packs and community servers).

## Open questions (what the user must decide)

1. Goal: a faithful port of tgstation content (roles, items, maps) or a new 3D game with the spirit of SS13 (like Stationeers and Barotrauma)? Everything else depends on this.
2. Camera perspective: angled top-down with rotation (SS3D), first/third person (Centration, SpessVR, Lethal Company) or VR? Do grid movement and tile-based construction remain?
3. Logic: reuse DM code (an OpenDream server or code translation) or write the core in C# from scratch? Are we ready to spend 1-2 weeks on a spike of both?
4. Session size: 50-150 players as in SS13, about 16 as in Barotrauma, or 4-6 co-op?
5. Distribution model: an open project (AGPL/MIT, community, community servers) or a commercial release (asset license, Steam)?
6. Team and funding: solo, a small team, recruiting contributors; are we ready to run an open community, documentation, bounties?
7. What from SS13 is the mandatory core: atmosphere and electricity simulation, medicine, social sabotage and roles, chemistry?
