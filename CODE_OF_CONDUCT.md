# Code of conduct

We want Anomaly Station to be a friendly place to work on a game together, whoever you are and whatever your
experience. This code applies to the project's spaces: this repository (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions) and
our [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/3QYKDaWVX3). The rules of a game server are up to that server's hosts.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone was new once.
- Criticise the work, not the person. Explain why, and suggest how to make it better.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and accept that the maintainers sometimes say no.
- Assume good intent. When you are not sure what someone meant, ask.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information (a real name, an address, contacts) without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or spam.

In-character content of the game itself (antagonists, violence on the station) is fine where it belongs; using it to
target real people is not.

## Enforcement

If someone breaks these rules, or you feel unsafe, tell a maintainer or a moderator on Discord in a private message. We
will look at every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and act in proportion: a warning, a temporary ban or a
permanent ban from the project's spaces. Maintainers who don't follow this code can be removed from the project.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
