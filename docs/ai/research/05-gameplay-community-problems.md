# 05. Gameplay and Social Problems of SS13: What We Must Not Lose

Research date: 2026-10-05. Direction: game design, social dynamics, moderation, the SS13 core.
Decision context (from `docs/ai/DECISIONS.md`): D3 open project under AGPLv3, D4 first-person view, D5 vertical slice first, D6 multiplayer for 30-100+ players.
Local facts about tgstation are taken from the read-only clone `../tgstation/` (configs and code); external facts are backed by pages that I actually opened. Confidence: high / medium / low.

## Summary

1. SS13 does not rest on graphics or on individual mechanics, but on a combination: a fragile shared station with interlinked systems, roles with real expertise and interdependence, hidden antagonists (social deduction), and players who write the stories themselves. This is what SS14 developers and players call the core. Losing any one element turns the game into "just a space sandbox."
2. The main pain points of the original are social, not technical: the learning-curve cliff, rounds that depend on antagonists, rules that prop up unfinished mechanics, admin overload, the split across RP levels, and staff toxicity. SS14 experiences these problems too; by Steam Charts it currently has the most visible online statistics among the remakes (⚠️ the wording "best in the niche" and the comparison with SS13 are not confirmed, see F36).
3. The most significant observation for us: the most serious threat to a mature open project in this niche is people, not code. In 2024-2026 SS14 went through admin burnout and a governance crisis (⚠️ corrected: after the letter from 34 staff members, the lead engine maintainer stepped back but did not leave for good, and later announced her return; see F21, F24). For D3 a governance and moderation model is needed in advance.
4. The first-person view has no successful (released and alive) precedent: Centration was abandoned in 2015; ⚠️ corrected: this is not the only first-person attempt, SpessVR is also first-person (desktop and VR) but has not yet reached even Early Access; the other 3D projects are either pre-alpha or top-down. Some of SS13's social mechanics (hidden identity, station overview, antagonist stealth, combat) change radically in an FPS and need separate design, not a straight port.
5. ERP and 18+: this is not a marginal topic but an industry risk (the BYOND hub declined to intervene; the SS14 hub dehubs servers, and CSAM was found in the Discord of one server). The policy must be decided before the first public build.

## Findings

### A. What is considered the core of SS13

**F1. The core is systemic depth plus social deduction.** Players describe SS13 as "Among Us with Dwarf Fortress complexity" (HN; ⚠️ corrected: the Dwarf Fortress wording was written by user Aromasin in reply to the Among Us comparison from ajvs, not by ajvs himself). The Game Developer analysis stresses: stories are born from modular systems (atmospherics, genetics, telecomms) that players combine themselves; roles have real mechanical depth, secret roles create information asymmetry. Sources: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=29108648 (via https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/items/29108648), https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/space-station-13-a-case-study-in-emergent-gameplay-and-system-heavy-design. Confidence: high.

**F2. The SS14 design document lists the same pillars.** Unpredictability ("no two rounds are alike"), a changeable station, interlinked intuitive systems, natural player interaction, dark comedy as the tone; the official server follows the principle "mechanics instead of rules". Source: https://docs.spacestation14.com/en/space-station-14/core-design.html. Confidence: high.

**F3. The context of "surviving on an already-built station" is critical.** An HN comment: imitators break when they replace desperate improvisation on someone else's poorly understood station with building from scratch (Minecraft-style). Source: https://hn.svelte.dev/item/35510067. Confidence: medium (the opinion of one user, but it matches F1).

**F4. "Mechanical density" is the main barrier for remakes.** The "remake curse" discussion points to accumulated content (many antagonists, departments) and players' attachment to existing servers. Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=29109017. For scale: in our tgstation clone there are 7476 `.dm` files in `code/` (7494 in the whole repository, including tools and interface), about 1.23 million lines (recounted: 1,228,877 lines across all `.dm`); antagonists 369 files (about 62 thousand lines), surgery+atmos+reagents+science about 122 thousand lines, 37 entries in `config/jobconfig.toml`, 93 event files. Confidence: high (local count, my estimate from `wc`).

**F5. In 2021 SS14 was criticized for lacking the depth of SS13** (surgery, organs, virology, circuits, supermatter) and for a "redundant" set of damage types; the community's response: SS14 is a spiritual successor, development is volunteer-driven. Source: https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/some-feedback-i-guess/12827. Confidence: medium (the thread dates from the early years, some of it may already be outdated).

### B. Learning curve and retention

**F6. Onboarding is a "cliff", help depends on people.** Players describe a steep curve (confirmed: a Level1Techs post about "Dwarf Fortress-level UI and learning curves"; ⚠️ the claim that a newcomer needs an acquaintance who already plays was not found in the checked fragment of that post); the SS14 forum notes that the game is hard for a newcomer to read. A PCGamesN author already wrote in 2013 that the game is "non-obvious" and that veterans do not forgive newcomers' mistakes. Sources: https://forum.level1techs.com/t/level1-space-station-13/142428, https://www.pcgamesn.com/indie/space-station-13-free-multiplayer-space-station-simulator-thats-mostly-about-paperwork. Confidence: high.

**F7. What servers do.** Paradise: a list of "safe" starting roles (Assistant, Cargo Technician, Janitor, Librarian, Bartender), a glossary, in-game mentors via F1. Goonstation: a built-in tutorial, advice for newcomers to avoid Security and not to call the shuttle. Sources: https://www.paradisestation.org/wiki/New_Players_Portal, https://wiki.ss13.co/Getting_Started. Confidence: high.

**F8. "Playground" servers work, but run into people limits.** SS14 Grasshopper ("babyjail") restricts entry by whitelist and to fewer than 50 hours; problems: veterans cannot teach their friends, command roles stay empty, the whitelist queue exceeds two weeks. The official starter servers are overcrowded at prime time. Ghost-role mentors for newcomers were proposed. Sources: https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/babyjail-and-the-future-of-grasshopper/10336, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/i-feel-like-theres-more-players-on-at-prime-times-than-there-are-servers-to-accommodate/21659, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/starter-role-pais/28636. Confidence: high.

**F9. Retention: there is inflow, outflow is fast.** According to BeeStation data, the largest surge of new players came from Sseth's video review in February-April 2019, after which the number of first connections fell back to normal. The tg admin (MrStonedOne) wrote on HN that newcomers arrive in the "high double digits" per day, but most do not survive to the end of the first week (⚠️ corrected: it used to say "over weeks"). Video virality led to an influx of newcomers who copied griefing tricks from the video; according to the post author, they were jailed or killed in the round (⚠️ corrected: the source says nothing about bans; the date of the viral video is not given in the Level1Techs post, the post is dated 3 May 2019). Sources: https://forums.beestation13.com/t/ss13-data-part-2/10983, https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=29108648, https://forum.level1techs.com/t/level1-space-station-13/142428. Confidence: medium (the first part is Bee data, the second is one person's words).

**F10. Gating heavy roles is the norm.** In tg, play hours and account age are set by default: for example AI 180 minutes and a 30-day account, Captain 180 minutes, Security 300 minutes and 7 days (the keys in `config/jobconfig.toml` are commented out, the values show the defaults). There are interviews for newcomers (`config/interviews.txt`), a panic bunker by living minutes (`config/config.txt`), an "Intern" mark on the ID. Criticism of hour gates: grind and "dark patterns"; achievements are proposed instead of hours. Sources: local configs, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/playtime-unlockables-discussion/26112. Confidence: high.

### C. Roles

**F11. Gimmick roles are contentious.** SS14 developers call them "an assistant in a costume", weakly integrated into the mechanics; they propose rotating light "joker roles" with template rooms. Source: https://docs.spacestation14.com/en/space-station-14/departments/service/proposals/joker_roles.html. Confidence: high.

**F12. Security is the main point of player burnout.** On SS14 LRP a player describes: constant random events plus waiting to "catch the antags"; space law forces letting obvious antags go; the final "in custody" counters provoke "hunting". Source: https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/my-thoughts-on-lrp-security/22580. Confidence: medium (one post, but it matches F15 and F16).

**F13. I did not find distributions of role preferences (which are overloaded, which are empty).** Indirectly: in tg, assistants have no slot limit (`Total Positions = -1`), command and combat roles are limited by hours. See Gaps.

### D. Antagonists and balance

**F14. The round often rests on antagonists, and this is a design defect.** SS14 thread: a "green shift" is perceived as a disaster; all antagonists oppose Security, so successful Security work kills the round's interest; PvE events and station goals are proposed (⚠️ clarification: station goals "as in another SS13 fork" were mentioned by one commenter, not by the thread author). BeeStation describes the same picture: 1-2 boring hours, then chaos at the end, with antagonists completing their objectives at the last minute. Sources: https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/antagonists-being-the-only-round-driving-force-is-not-the-best-idea/25101, https://forums.beestation13.com/t/key-issues-development-focus-how-things-will-change/27500. Confidence: high.

**F15. Antagonists are designed around pillars.** The SS14 document: spark, escalation, "full-time job", back-and-forth, distinguishable evidence, accessibility, "fun to lose". Criticism of the wizard: instantly removes command and security, has no progression and no counterplay; in SS14 it was removed as a game mode. Sources: https://docs.spacestation14.com/en/space-station-14/round-flow/antagonists.html, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/wizard-antag-violates-almost-all-of-the-antagonist-design-pillars/24719. Confidence: high.

**F16. The traditional traitor with an objective checklist pushes toward stealth and "round-removal".** Complaints: victory is checked at the end, failure is expensive, the crew has the escalation advantage, AI and borgs have no real "neutrality". The tg design "Progression Traitors": fewer starting TC, items unlocked through progression, objective selection like contracts, final objectives (for example, a zombie outbreak), each objective leaves traces for Security, experienced players reach the finale in 80-110 minutes. Sources: https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/the-big-traitor-feedback-post/25207?page=3, https://hackmd.io/@tgstation/ry1Z_3SPF. Confidence: high.

**F17. Rules prop up mechanics.** SS14 rule 2.9, "do not go beyond your objectives", holds, according to an admin, because otherwise almost all antagonists would "annihilate the station". Players believe that because of the rules, real danger and the feeling of a "unique and hellish shift" disappeared, and that the rules cover holes in medicine and combat. In October 2025 Paradise cut the core rules from about 9000 to 5300 words and removed the obligation to balance around Security and antags; players complain about the "straitjacket" of objectives. Sources: https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/my-personal-veiws-on-the-game-extremely-massive-post/25616, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/thoughts-on-the-mechanical-lore-direction-of-ss14/25070, https://www.paradisestation.org/forum/topic/26546-paradise-station-rules-changes/, https://www.paradisestation.org/forum/topic/26215-admins-and-antag-conduct/. Confidence: high.

**F18. In tgstation antagonists are driven by "Dynamic" with an admin veto.** In `config/dynamic.toml` there are five chaos levels with weights: Greenshift 2, Low 8, Low-Medium 46, Medium-High 36, High 10; the rulesets are roundstart, light and heavy midround (time thresholds of 30 and 60 minutes for Low, Low-Medium and Medium-High; ⚠️ corrected: for High Chaos the thresholds are 20 and 30 minutes, for Greenshift 30 and 60), and latejoin; population thresholds are 25 / 40 / 50 players (recounted from `config/dynamic.toml`); in the code there is a 15-second window in which an admin (if online) can cancel or reroll a midround threat (`code/controllers/subsystem/dynamic/dynamic.dm`). There are 11 roundstart antagonist roles and dozens of midround/ghost roles (`code/__DEFINES/role_preferences.dm`). For each tg antagonist a connection to the "policy config" is required (JSON with the rules text for the role, `../tgstation/.github/guides/POLICYCONFIG.md`). Confidence: high (local code).

### E. Metagaming, griefing, rules

**F19. The rules vocabulary is shared across servers.** Metagaming (using knowledge the character does not have, including exchange via Discord), powergaming, NLR (after death and resuscitation you must not remember the killer), conflict escalation, a ban on "hunting valids" without a reason, and at Wizden a "golden rule": an admin may deviate from any rule for the sake of the round. Goonstation: griefing is prohibited but allowed for antagonists; sexual content is strictly prohibited. Sources: https://paradisestation.org/wiki/index.php/A_Crash_Course_In_Roleplaying, https://hackmd.io/@Peptide90/MRP-Rules, https://wiki.ss13.co/Rules. Confidence: high.

**F20. FPS games with social deduction have the same problem.** TTT in Garry's Mod requires moderation of "random kills" (RDM), otherwise deduction turns into a shootout. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trouble_in_Terrorist_Town. Confidence: medium (an analogy, not SS13).

### F. Admin load and toxicity

**F21. Moderation is the main bottleneck.** SS14/Wizden: the appeals queue piles up for months, one former admin says he processed 500 appeals himself, the admin application queue reached 1-2 years, the staff complaints queue is "unacceptably long", and the admin team has largely burned out (confirmed: a post from October 2024); an "admin strike" (walkout), after which appeals were auto-rejected, is mentioned by one admin in a thread dated 15 December 2025 (⚠️ the year of the strike, "2025", is not confirmed: the source does not name the date, and source 14251 already in 2024 writes about the loss of many admins; the auto-rejection is confirmed only by the words of one admin, and entry 14251 itself speaks of an accelerated appeals procedure, not of auto-rejection). "500 appeals" was written in a comment by user lonesoldier55 in thread 14346, it is not an official metric. Measures: an appeals team, application windows once every couple of months, note expiry. Sources: https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/update-on-measures-taken-with-wizards-den-game-ban-appeals/14251, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/new-method-for-admin-applications/27831, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/public-comments-on-the-staff-complaints-process/12926, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/request-for-feedback-changes-to-the-appeals-policy/14346, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/my-personal-veiws-on-the-game-extremely-massive-post/25616. Confidence: high.

**F22. Admin policy enshrines "minimal intervention".** Wizden: intervene only when necessary, "mechanics instead of rules", an admin deadmins for the duration of play; Goobstation: strikes for admins, three strikes lead to removal, separate IC and OOC punishments, a round can be ended artificially. Sources: https://docs.spacestation14.com/en/community/admin/wizards-den-admin-policy.html, https://docs.goobstation.com/en/community/admin/goob-station-admin-policy.html. Confidence: high.

**F23. Toxicity hits people and projects.** Paradise's long-time staff member Fox McCloud left the project in 2023 (announcement on 6 August, departure from 15 September); ⚠️ corrected: as the reason he names a sharp decline in his own involvement, not harassment; he describes the harassment campaign and a hostile Reddit as a "dark period" of 2015-2017, not as a reason for leaving in 2023. Confirmed: in 2014 Paradise nearly fell apart (online in single digits). The SS14 server Vulture degraded, according to players' accounts, due to staff neglect. The SS14 hub gave a server a strike for harassment of community members. Sources: https://paradisestation.org/forum/topic/24267-goodbye-farewell-amen, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/vulture-feels-pointless/27157, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/2023-08-21-strike-against-apocalypse-station-harassment-of-community-members/5542. Confidence: medium (participants' testimony).

**F24. The SS14 governance crisis (2026).** ⚠️ Corrected against the primary source: a letter demanding reforms (governance, accountability, control over infrastructure), signed by 34 staff members (30 named and 4 anonymous), was published on 20 February 2026 on the internal forum. The lead engine maintainer (PJB) did not "leave" but stepped back: she handed Discord ownership to another person and "almost stopped following", while, in her words, continuing to pay for the infrastructure; she later wrote that she had returned, and stated that she would demote the staff members who signed the letter if they did not apologize (this is her own version of events, not independent confirmation). A "state of affairs" thread was opened on 3 May 2026 (the "where has PJB been" thread is dated 2 May 2026); questions surfaced about the handover of Patreon, PayPal, Cloudflare hosting, Steam, GitHub and the Robust Toolbox backend; proposals are to separate ownership, code and moderation. Sources: https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/where-has-pjb-been/27331, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/wizden-current-state-of-affairs/27379. Confidence: medium (participants' reports, the outcome is unknown). For D3 this is the main lesson about bus factor: key infrastructure and revenue were tied to a single person.

### G. Splitting servers by RP

**F25. Definitions.** LRP: mechanics and jokes come first, RP is minimal; MRP: the character behaves as if alive, but the rules are softer; HRP: strict character consistency. On HRP newcomers more often meet patient helpers, on LRP "everyone wants to kill you", which also teaches. Sources: https://paradisestation.org/wiki/index.php/A_Crash_Course_In_Roleplaying, https://forums.aurorastation.org/topic/13065-learning-to-play-ss13-hrp-v-lrp-servers/. Confidence: high.

**F26. The terms are blurry, the community fragments.** "MRP" means something different on every server; players drift between LRP, HRP forks and other games; the population of the MRP server Salamander cyclically fluctuates from "dead pop" to overflow, with the reasons being a drop in RP quality, content stagnation, and friends leaving. Sources: https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/how-do-did-you-transition-to-mrp-servers/27232, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/is-salamander-dying/21351/6. Confidence: high.

**F27. A population split kills servers.** BeeStation closed the LRP server Golden on 30 July 2021: falling dynamics, tribalism between the HRP (Sage) and LRP bases, the burden on the code of maintaining two rule sets; ⚠️ corrected: 70 players on Sage is an intentional hard limit that Bee does not intend to raise (so as not to repeat the split), not a technical ceiling that they "hit"; the announcement also says that Bee will not support servers with low or zero RP. Source: https://forums.beestation13.com/t/golden-honey-no-longer-being-produced-by-beestation-inc/14033. Confidence: high.

**F28. One codebase serves different genres.** The subreddit's server list (an undated snapshot) includes Colonial Marines, Civ13 (historical eras), SinguloStation (multi-day rounds); in tg RP is optional, and some roles are closed by hours. Source: https://redlib.groet-infra.nl/r/SS13/wiki/server_list. Confidence: medium (the date of the snapshot is unknown).

### H. ERP and 18+

**F29. This is an industry risk.** In January 2021 on the BYOND forum a post author claimed that about a third of the populated hub servers contain a sexual element (Skyrat, Citadel, Hyper Station and others are named); Lummox JR replied that servers with warnings and without explicit material on the hub page fit within BYOND's terms, and if minors join such a server, the responsibility lies with them and their parents (⚠️ clarified: this is not "BYOND does not intervene" in general); the author counted about 10 of 32 servers (Skyrat, Citadel, Hyper Station and others are named). A thread about delisting servers that violate the terms (⚠️ this is a different, earlier thread, July 2020) is marked "Not Feasible"; the January 2021 thread has no such tag. The phrase "the hub belongs to everyone and no one" belongs to participant Ter13, not to Lummox JR. Sources: https://www.byond.com/forum/post/2649633 (January-March 2021), http://www.byond.com/forum/post/2590597 (July 2020). Confidence: high (the thread facts), medium (the "third" claim is the author's words).

**F30. The SS14 hub has explicit rules and sanctions.** Servers with ERP must carry an 18+ tag and remove minors, three strikes lead to exclusion. Cases: Spacious Station (31 January 2025), Red Space (12 August 2025; besides the tag, CSAM was found in the NSFW channel of the server's Discord), the "0+ no rules erp" server (31 October 2025). Sources: https://docs.spacestation14.com/en/community/space-wizards-hub-rules.html, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/2025-01-31-dehubbing-of-spacious-station/16702, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/2025-08-12-dehubbing-of-red-space/23056, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/2025-10-31-dehubbing-of-0-no-rules-erp-server/25029. Confidence: high.

**F31. Official servers prohibit ERP, and it is costly to moderate.** Reasons in the discussions: a 16+ audience with minors, most people do not want sex in a "funny space game", the difficulty of moderation; the harsh "zero tolerance" policy is criticized (⚠️ corrected: in thread 9064, June 2024, the criticism is aimed at excessive harshness toward players; the argument "it overloads admins with appeals" was not found in this thread and remains unverified); confirmed: by an admin vote on 6 February 2024 the policy on ERP and sexual content was split and clarified. Sources: https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/erp-is-against-the-rules-why-exactly/4764, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/erp-zero-tolerance-enforcement-context-and-whether-such-a-heavy-handed-approach-is-actually-needed/9064. Confidence: medium.

**F32. A license does not give control over forks.** ⚠️ Clarified against the primary source: in the Citadel document (a project document for a new server, not about Sector XIV itself) it is said that "furry" SS13 servers differ little from each other in gameplay, and attempts to stand out are largely futile because of the nature of AGPL; Citadel names as its solution moving some unique features into closed code while the shared repository stays open. This is the opinion of the authors of one fork, and the wording "almost useless" in the heading was my paraphrase. Source: https://hackmd.io/@deathride58/H1X4CYyFj. Confidence: medium. For D3 this means: AGPL protects openness but not uniqueness, and forks may react by closing parts of the code (whether this is permissible under AGPL was not checked and is outside the scope of this direction). Separately: in SS13 "the hub belongs to everyone and no one" (from the BYOND thread above) and therefore could not remove anything.

### I. Round length, events, death

**F33. Round pacing is a design problem.** SS14 describes "random" events as an unpredictable tempo and proposes a Game Director: it measures the station's "chaos" (power, atmosphere, wounded, anomalies) and picks events in "beats" of about five minutes. Meteors in SS14 were criticized as a purely reactive threat without counterplay: engineers are left with no time for creativity, and the round can be ended by a dice roll. Sources: https://docs.spacestation14.com/en/space-station-14/round-flow/proposals/game-director.html, https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/current-state-of-meteors-genuinenly-makes-the-game-unplayable/9274. Confidence: high. In tg there is a `station_goals` module (BSA, DNA vault, meteor shield) as PvE goals (local).

**F34. Round length.** I did not obtain direct figures for tg/Goon (Gaps). Indirectly: about 80-110 minutes to the finale of an advanced traitor (F16); BeeStation calls the first 1-2 hours boring (F14); on Paradise one player wrote in January 2014 that newcomers barely endure an hour and a half (a thread dated 25 January 2014, one person's opinion, https://paradisestation.org/forum/topic/443-full-shift-marathon); the post-round window in tg is 90 seconds, the lobby 120 seconds (`config/config.txt`). Confidence: low-medium.

**F35. Death and spectators.** In tg respawn is off by default (`ALLOW_RESPAWN 0`), the dead remain ghosts and can take ghost roles (locally there are `mob_spawn/ghost_roles`, `deathmatch`, `mafia` for lobby entertainment). The SS14 discussion: easy resuscitation removes the crew's "fear", but a long wait as a ghost is annoying, especially if there are no ghost roles. Source: https://forum.spacestation14.com/t/crew-should-be-made-more-afraid-and-round-removal-more-acceptable-in-certain-cases/23582. Confidence: medium.

### J. Statistics and the fate of remakes

**F36. ⚠️ SS14 now has a larger presence than SS13: NOT CONFIRMED.** Confirmed (Steam Charts, rechecked): September 2026 averaged 2609 concurrent players, peak 4225; all-time peak 4695 in April 2026; monthly averages over the last 12 months range from 2385 (September 2025) to 3038 (August 2026), so "steadily 2500-3000" is true only approximately. Caveat: Steam Charts counts only the Steam client, while SS14 can also be launched through its own launcher, so the real online is probably higher (not checked). The comparison with SS13 relies only on a post by user zeeb from 5 April 2023 in a Steam thread (1754 vs 1743) without a source for the BYOND number; this is not data but an unverifiable remark from three years ago. There are no current figures for SS13 (see Gaps), so the claims "best statistics in the niche" and "more than SS13" cannot be used as justification for decisions. Sources: https://steamcharts.com/app/1482520, https://steamcommunity.com/app/1255460/discussions/0/3826411948439795409/. Confidence: high (the SS14 figures on Steam), unsupported (the comparison with SS13).

**F37. The SS14 path is long.** According to Wikipedia, development started around 2011, the source code was opened in January 2015, a new phase began in May 2017, Steam Playtest ran from February 2021, and it has its own C# engine, Robust Toolbox. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Station_14. Confidence: medium.

**F38. 3D remakes.** Centration: first person, Unity and then Unreal; in the Unity version multiplayer worked, in the Unreal version it did not; officially abandoned and removed from Steam in 2015 (clarified). Primulus: top-down 3D by two Baystation12 developers, a tech demo, then a failed Kickstarter. ⚠️ Ion is not an SS13 remake: it is an original space MMO by Dean Hall, merely inspired by SS13; cancelled on 7 March 2017, the reason, per Hall, being the dependence on Improbable's technology (exclude it from the list of remakes, use it with caution as an argument about "remake failures"). RE:SS3D: pre-alpha on Unity with Mirror, an angled top-down camera with rotation, a simplified "TG core", "a couple of years" until it is playable; earlier the project was abandoned by its sole developer and revived by the community. SpessVR: ⚠️ corrected: "early access" is wrong, the Steam page says the game is preparing for Early Access and is not yet available, the date is not announced; desktop and VR modes, both first-person; the developers explicitly state that they do not aim for feature parity; closed source, free. Unitystation fell behind SS14 in pace (confirmed by one user via the commit count for February 2023: 549 vs 59; the Steam thread "ded?" from December 2022; the current state is unknown). Sources: https://nsv.beestation13.com/wiki/List_of_remakes, https://www.pcgamesn.com/dayz/dean-hall-mmo-ion-cancelled, https://ss3d.space/faq/, https://store.steampowered.com/app/898200/SpessVR/, https://steamcommunity.com/app/801140/discussions/0/3717188244459307205. Confidence: high (facts), medium (interpretation).

## Implications for the 3D port

### The core without which this stops being SS13 (carry over)

1. **A shared fragile station**: power, atmosphere, doors, depressurization, which anyone can break and repair; play on an already-built station, not building from scratch (F1-F3).
2. **Roles with expertise and interdependence**: engineer, doctor, Security, command; skills matter, they are not cosmetic (F1, F11).
3. **Hidden antagonists and social deduction** with escalation and traces (F15-F16).
4. **Players write the stories**: RP, formal hierarchies, factions; the mechanics must support this, not replace it (F1, F25).
5. **Death that is meaningful but not elimination**: ghosts, ghost roles, spectating (F35).
6. **The tone of dark comedy** and the right to absurdity (F2).
7. **Forkability**: the rule set as config, like the policy config in tg (F18, F28).

### First-person (D4) and multiplayer (D6) specifics

- **Hidden identity** in tg is built on "Unknown" instead of a name when the face is covered / without an ID (`code/modules/mob/living/carbon/human/human_helpers.dm`). In an FPS the player sees clothing and a silhouette; we need to decide what disguise and ID use to replace the "name above the head", otherwise deduction breaks. The conclusion is mine, there are no direct sources (confidence low).
- **Overview**: from above a "cluster" of the crew is visible; in an FPS Security sees a narrow cone, and it is easier for an antagonist to hide. Cameras and radio gain more weight; antagonist balance will have to be recalculated (low).
- **First-person precedents** (⚠️ added during verification): SpessVR (desktop and VR, closed source, Early Access not released) and Centration (abandoned in 2015) show that a first-person SS13 has been tried at least twice; study SpessVR as the closest analog of D4, but its "unreleased" status means that none of the projects found has experience with live first-person multiplayer for 30-100 players.
- **Combat**: if it is aim-based, newcomers and non-fighters lose, and "hunting valids" and griefing become more expensive to moderate (an analog of RDM in TTT, F20). A policy is needed: how far combat depends on skill and how non-combat roles are protected.
- **Voice**: proximity voice turns metagaming, harassment and ERP into things that are hard to moderate through recordings. Text in tg is well logged and filtered (`config/word_filter.toml`, 160 files in `code/modules/admin`, about 31 thousand lines). The decision must be made explicitly (open question).
- **Population**: SS13-like games have a "sweet spot" of 30-50 players (comments about Salamander), 70 players is Sage's intentional limit (⚠️ not a "ceiling they hit", see F27), tg Dynamic thresholds are 25-50 players (confirmed); according to one commenter, Salamander fluctuated from 30-50 in the evening hours to being full at 80/80 and "dead pop". The D6 goal of "30-100+" is above typical. For 100+ the interest and the load must be checked; with a small population a server enters a "death loop" (Golden, Vulture). A low-population mode is needed (fewer slots, bots or role filling, ghost roles).
- **Moderation as a product**: in tg the moderation infrastructure (SQL bans, known_alts, stickyban, ipintel, panic bunker, interviews) takes up a noticeable share of the code. The slice needs at least a minimum: ahelp, notes, bans, appeals, a chat filter, an interview/newcomer gate.

### What is reasonable to take for the vertical slice (D5)

One station, roles: Assistant/Engineer/Medic/Security/Captain (minimal command), atmosphere and power, one antagonist with progression and traces, a minimal-size event director (2-3 events with counterplay), notes/bans, a tutorial. The director and the antagonist are designed around the SS14 pillars (F15) from the very start, not added later.

### What to avoid

- Copying server rules as is: they accumulated over years and are themselves contradictory (F17).
- Building the round only on antagonists and random reactive events (F14, F33).
- Postponing the ERP/18+ policy and moderation to "after release" (F29-F31, F21).
- Building the project around one maintainer and one owner of the infrastructure (F24).
- Aiming for parity with SS13 in scope: the only "surviving" remakes have been going for a decade (F37), and the 3D projects do not chase parity (F38).

## Open questions (what the owner must decide)

1. **Server model**: one official flagship with a "light MRP" profile plus community servers that are open, or several modes right away? Who hosts and pays?
2. **Content and age policy**: a ban on ERP in the official build and on the official hub? An 18+ tag and mandatory removal of minors for community servers (like the SS14 hub)? Age target (13+/16+/18+)?
3. **Master server list**: do we make an official hub with rules and strikes (like the Space Wizards hub), or leave it "to everyone and no one"?
4. **Voice chat**: is proximity voice needed (immersion, but hard to moderate) or only text and radio at the start?
5. **First-person combat model**: aim-based shooting and melee, or a model dependent on stats/intents? How do we protect non-fighters and newcomers?
6. **Antagonists in the slice**: which one first (a traitor with progression?) and what round philosophy (one main antagonist per round, as at Bee, or several)? Do we allow round-removal and within what limits?
7. **Round length and pacing**: a target time (for example, 60-90 minutes)? An event director in the slice or a simple set of events?
8. **Death**: resuscitation and ghost roles, no respawn, as in tg? What do dead players do in a 100-player round?
9. **Onboarding**: how deep a tutorial, is a separate "kids" server needed, mentors via ghost roles, hour gates for command and Security?
10. **Project governance**: who owns the domain, the repository, Discord, Patreon/hosting? Do we separate ownership, code and moderation? Is there a code of conduct and rules for staff (strikes for admins)?
11. **Minimum population**: what do we do when 5-10 people are online (bots, reduced roles, a "sandbox" mode)? Is the 100+ goal realistic for the start?
12. **What we count as "core parity"**: which systems are mandatory (atmosphere, power, medicine, chemistry, AI and laws, genetics?), and which can be discarded?
13. **Tone and universe**: do we inherit Nanotrasen and tg's humor or create our own universe (a question of IP and assets, see D3)?
