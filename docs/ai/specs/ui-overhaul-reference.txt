🎯 1. Core Architectural Recommendation
Prioritize a context-driven HUD with a three-zone Tab overlay (Left: Expanded Chat — Center: Character Doll & Equipped Gear — Right: Dual Stacked Containers) paired with a 3-Tier Hybrid Item UI where 65% of station machines render automatically from JSON/metadata schemas. Keeping the live first-person viewport clean while docking external loot/strip panels exclusively on the right guarantees chat is never blocked during high-stakes social or combat moments.

🖥️ 2. Screen Layout & Dual-Inventory System
To ensure stripping, searching, and crate transfers never obscure the chat feed, split the Tab overlay into three strict horizontal columns. When Tab is closed, collapse all center and container panels down to lightweight peripheral status anchors.

Screen Column

Closed HUD State (Live 1P Gameplay)

Open Tab Overlay State (Cursor Unlocked)

Key UX Rules & Thresholds

Left Zone (0%–28% W)

Compact 6-line chat & radio box at bottom-left; background fades to 0% opacity after 8s idle

Full-height scrollable chat log, channel filter toggles, and whisper/radio input bar

Preserves 100% chat visibility during looting, surgery, or stripping

Center Zone (28%–64% W)

Minimal center crosshair with 2-line contextual hover prompt; bottom hotbar + Hand slots

Barotrauma / Neo Scavenger anatomical doll surrounded by clothing slots, pockets, and belt

Keeps drag-and-drop travel under 300px between hands, pockets, and equipment

Right Zone (64%–100% W)

Compact status Health Doll + critical alerts (Internals, Gravity, Temp) at middle-right

Vertically stacked dual containers: Top-Right = Your Backpack; Bottom-Right = Target Container / Stripped Player

Supports universal drag-and-drop plus Shift+LMB instant transfer; auto-closes target panel beyond 1.8m

For stripping or searching other crewmates in 3D, render radial timer overlays directly onto target equipment slots (e.g., 4.0s for jumpsuits, 2.0s for pockets) so progress is immediately readable without adding floating modal bars. Add a "Nearby Floor / Surface" strip below the external container slot so small dropped items (syringes, scalpels, IDs) are easy to grab without pixel-hunting in 3D.

🤲 3. Hotbar, Active Hands & Firearms ADS Matrix
Binding 1–9 to items buried inside backpacks undermines /tg/station storage balance. Enforce a physicalized Project Zomboid / Tarkov rig rule: only items currently stored in Pockets, Belt/Toolbelt, Suit Storage, or ID/PDA slots can be bound to hotbar keys 1–9.

Control / Slot

Specification

Design Rationale

Left & Right Hand Slots

Oversized anchor slots flanking 1–9 (Left Hand on left, Right Hand on right) with high-contrast highlight on Active Hand

Retains iconic two-handed SS13 item juggling; X (or Q) toggles Active Hand instantly

Quick-Swap (1–9)

Pressing 1–9 moves the bound belt/pocket item into your Active Hand; pressing the same number again stows it back to its rig slot

Keeps interaction logic consistent—every world interaction always checks what is physically in your Active Hand

Combat Mode Toggle (C or F)

Binary HUD state banner (Peaceful vs. Combat) adapting modern /tg/station Combat Mode

Prevents accidental tool attacks when repairing walls or opening unpowered doors

Contextual Right-Click & ADS

If holding an Aimable firearm: Hold RMB for ADS (move Rack Slide / Chamber to R or Alt+RMB). If holding non-firearm / empty hand: RMB executes Secondary Use or Shove/Disarm

Resolves the classic FPS ADS vs. SS13 secondary-interaction conflict cleanly

🩺 4. Health Doll, V.A.T.S. Limb Targeting & 1P Visibility
Because multiplayer SS13 cannot pause time, implement your V.A.T.S. concept as a real-time "Anatomical Focus" sub-mesh highlighter so players can identify alien/non-humanoid anatomy (xenomorphs, space carp, golems, megafauna) on the fly for both combat and surgery.

V.A.T.S. Limb Selector (Hold Alt or MMB)

Raycasts up to 15m, highlighting anatomical sub-meshes with floating tags (Head, Torso, L-Tendril / Leg)

Releasing Alt while aiming at a sub-mesh locks that limb on your HUD targeting doll until changed

Damage HUD & Screen FX

Directional blood vignette for Brute, heat-wave border for Burn, cyan frost creep for OxyLoss, and audio muffling below 20% HP

1P Wrestling, Grabs & Carries

Edge-of-screen grip brackets color-coded by /tg/ grab tier: Yellow (Passive), Orange (Aggressive + sway), Red (Chokehold + tunnel vision)

Display a centered [Space / WASD] Break Free stamina bar; during Fireman Carry, tilt victim camera 25° with over-shoulder view

1P Hallucinations

Client-only UI deception mirroring /tg/'s screwy_health_doll, inhand_fake_item, and fake_chat hooks

Briefly flash fake severed limbs on the Health Doll, swap held item viewmodels for 3s, or spawn phantom off-screen speech pills

💬 5. Contextual Prompts, Spatial Chat & Radio Readability
In 3D, players lose top-down situational awareness of people speaking behind them and hidden item combinations. Combine a two-part crosshair verb prompt with clamped off-screen speech pills and Starlight SS14-style keyword highlighting.

Communication / Tip Layer

UI Behavior

Readability Thresholds & Styling

Hover + Held Item Tips

Dynamic prompt below-right of reticle updates based on Active Hand item + target object + Combat Mode

Format: [LMB] Weld Hull Breach / [RMB] Slice. Holding F1 expands a card listing all verbs for your held item

On-Screen & Off-Screen Local Chat

3D bubbles above heads inside 100° FOV; clamped edge-of-screen directional pills with arrows when speakers are behind you

Scale font opacity/size from 0m–10m; whispers render italicized within 2m only

Department Radio & Highlights

Color-coded channel tags ([SEC] Red, [ENG] Orange, [MED] Cyan, [SCI] Purple, [CMD] Gold) in bottom-left log

Auto-highlight Character Name, Job Title, Department, and Syndicate Codewords with colored pill badges and soft audio ticks

1P Holder: Scrolling OLED text on the 3D radio screen + chat log. 3D World (<4m): Small floating [145.9] "..." pop-out text above the physical radio prop

📟 6. Item & Machine UI Strategy (3-Tier Hybrid)
Rebuilding hundreds of /tg/station TGUI windows by hand will stall production, whereas forcing everything into generic menus kills immersion. Split all item and machine interfaces across three strict production tiers:

Tier

Coverage

Architecture

Target Items

Tier 1: Diegetic 3D Screen

~25%

Live UI texture rendered straight onto the 1P viewmodel; hold I (Inspect) to bring close to camera without unlocking cursor

Health Analyzer, Gas Analyzer, T-Ray Scanner, Radiation Geiger Counter

Tier 2: Metadata Schema UI

~65%

Single TGUI-style JSON/metadata renderer generating standardized cards, sliders, reagent bars, and buttons automatically

APCs, Air Alarms, Vending Machines, Autolathes, Cryo/Sleeper Pods

Tier 3: Bespoke Custom UI

~10%

Hand-designed flagship interfaces with custom layouts, mini-games, or node graphs

PDA & Crew Manifest, ChemMaster, Surgery Console, Telecomms, Nuclear Bomb

⚠️ 7. Critical UX Risks & Guardrails
Risk

Impact in First-Person 3D

Design Rule

Cursor-Lock Ambush

Full-screen opaque menus blind players to traitors sneaking up behind them

Never blur the center 3D viewport; auto-close machine/loot panels on taking damage or moving >1.5m

Radio Scroll Overload

High-pop Common Radio (;) pushes local in-room speech off-screen in seconds

Give Local Speech a distinct white pill outline in the log and add a 1-click Local Only mini-tab

Motion Sickness in Grabs/Crits

Aggressive camera ragdolling in 1P triggers severe nausea

Keep player horizon locked upright during trips/stuns—communicate knockdowns via lowered camera height + screen vignette instead of spinning the camera
